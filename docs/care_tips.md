# General wellness tips

## Hydration
Sip water regularly through the day if that is part of your usual routine. If you have been given specific fluid guidance by a clinician, follow that guidance.

## Gentle movement
Choose comfortable movement that fits your routine, such as a short walk or gentle stretching. Stop if you feel unwell and seek help when needed.

## Sleep routine
A steady bedtime and a calm wind-down routine may make it easier to rest. Keep the routine comfortable and suited to your preferences.

## Staying connected
Calling a friend or family member, sharing a meal, or joining a familiar activity can help you feel connected during the day.

## Balanced meals
Regular meals can make daily routines easier to follow. Log what you ate if you would like to keep track; this companion does not provide nutrition or medical advice.
